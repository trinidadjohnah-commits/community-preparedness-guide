FEATURE 2

Emergency Kit Guide

Choose supplies that fit your household's needs and keep them together in an easy-to-carry place.

💧

Drinking water and food

Water and non-perishable food can help meet basic needs if regular supplies are interrupted.

🔦

Flashlight and batteries

A working flashlight can provide light during a power outage. Keep spare batteries with it.

📄

Important documents

Protected copies can be easier to bring and refer to if you need to leave home.

🧼

First-aid and personal supplies

Basic first-aid, hygiene, and needed personal items can help your household manage while away.

📻

Battery-powered radio

A radio may help you hear official updates if phone or internet service is unavailable.

🔋

Charged power bank

A charged power bank can help keep a mobile phone powered when outlets are unavailable.

FEATURE 3

General Evacuation Reminders

  • Follow official evacuation announcements and instructions from local authorities.
  • Learn the evacuation routes and centers designated by local authorities for your area.
  • When told to evacuate, take essential supplies and important documents if it is safe to do so.
  • Plan how to assist household members who may need support, when it is safe to help.

Safety note: This guide does not provide real-time hazard information. Check official government and local disaster-management channels for current guidance.
