# Laboratory Activity #4: Prompt, Observe, Refine

## Task 1 — Project Planning

**Innovation Sector:** Disaster Risk Reduction and Climate Change Adaptation

**Local Challenge:** Residents may not always know what to prepare before a disaster, which emergency supplies to include in a kit, or what general evacuation reminders to follow.

**Target Users:** Residents and households in Marinduque, especially individuals and families who want a simple preparedness guide.

**Proposed Project:** Community Preparedness Guide

**Project Description:** A simple, responsive website that helps community members review preparedness tasks, understand what to include in an emergency kit, and read general evacuation reminders.

**Main Features:**
1. Interactive emergency preparedness checklist with progress tracking.
2. Emergency kit guide listing useful supplies.
3. General evacuation reminders for community preparedness.

**Expected Benefit:** The website can help residents organize basic preparedness information in one accessible place. It is an educational guide and does not replace official emergency announcements or instructions from local authorities.

**Observation:**

Copilot provided a simple website using three beginner-friendly files: `index.html`, `style.css`, and `script.js`. It included an introduction, an interactive preparedness checklist, an emergency kit guide, and evacuation reminders. It also explained that the website does not require additional libraries or setup. Additionally, Copilot fixed the checklist markup so completed items display a strikethrough style. Overall, the response was clear and easy to understand, and it provided the basic features needed for the Community Preparedness Guide website.
